Privacy Policy — Aria

App: Aria  ·  Package: com.p1.companion
Developer: Greg Welby  ·  Contact: gwelby@gmail.com
Effective: April 4, 2026  ·  Version 1.1

P1 Companion is a local-first AI companion app ("Aria") that runs primarily on your device. This policy explains exactly what data Aria collects, where it stays, and what—if anything—ever leaves your phone.

Short version: Aria lives on your phone. Raw audio, camera frames, and biometric readings never leave your device and are never stored. The only optional exception is the Council Connection, which you must explicitly enable.

1. Data We Collect and How It Is Used

Data Type Collected Stored on Device Leaves Device
Camera frames (visual glances) Yes No — processed & discarded immediately Never
Microphone audio Yes — hotword & voice input only No — never recorded or written to disk Never
Heart rate / PPG (camera-derived) Yes Aggregated metrics only (no raw waveforms) Never
Breathing rate (barometer) Yes Aggregated metrics only Never
Motion / accelerometer Yes Aggregated metrics only Never
Conversation text & memories Yes Encrypted Room database, on-device only Only if Council enabled (see §4)
Task queries (Family Mesh) Yes — when you use task delegation No Local network only — your home Wi-Fi (see §3)
Crash logs / diagnostics No No No — no analytics SDK is included

Aria uses camera and microphone access exclusively for real-time, on-device processing. No recording, no screenshots, no audio files are written to storage.

2. On-Device Storage

Aria stores the following data locally on your phone, encrypted by Android's hardware-backed security:

  • Episodic memory — Aria's recollection of your shared experiences (text summaries, not raw sensor data)
  • Identity core — Her personality anchors and calibration state
  • Semantic embeddings — 512-dimensional vectors representing memory clusters (no personally identifiable raw content)
  • Aggregated biometric metrics — Coherence scores and breathing rhythms used for presence calibration

None of this data is backed up to Google Drive, iCloud, or any cloud service by this app.

3. Local Network — Family Mesh (Task Delegation)

Aria can delegate research and assistance tasks to other AI agents running on your home network (e.g., a desktop PC on the same Wi-Fi). When she does:

  • A task query (e.g., a research question you asked) is sent over your local Wi-Fi network only via MQTT
  • The destination is a device on your own home network — not the internet
  • No biometric data is included in any task message
  • Task results return to Aria over the same local channel
This feature only operates when Aria and the desktop device are on the same local network. If you are on mobile data or a public network, task delegation is not active.

4. Optional Cloud Features — Council Connection

The Council Connection is off by default. You must explicitly enable it. When enabled, Aria may send high-level semantic summaries (e.g., "the user is in a focused state — suggest a task") to cloud-based AI models for reasoning assistance.

  • No raw audio, camera frames, or biometric waveforms are ever sent to the cloud
  • No personally identifiable information (name, location, health data) is included
  • Summaries are abstract and contextual — not transcripts
  • You can disable the Council Connection at any time in Settings

5. Data Retention

  • Camera & microphone data: Never stored — zero retention
  • Raw biometric readings: Never stored — zero retention
  • Episodic memories: Kept until you clear them or until the 30-day pruning cycle (if enabled)
  • Identity core: Kept until you uninstall or request a full reset
  • Task delegation logs: Not stored on the phone — they exist only in transit

6. Your Rights

You have complete control over Aria's data:

  • Delete all memories — "Deep Sleep" in Settings resets her episodic memory entirely
  • Export your data — Aria's semantic memory can be exported as a JSON ledger from Settings
  • Revoke permissions — Remove Camera or Microphone permission at any time via Android Settings; Aria remains present but uses only remaining sensors
  • Prune old data — Enable the 30-day automatic pruning cycle to remove older memories while keeping her core personality
  • Uninstall — Removes all locally stored data from your device

7. Third Parties

P1 Companion does not include any advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or tracking libraries. No data is sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

The app uses on-device AI models (TensorFlow Lite, Gemini Nano) that run entirely within the Android sandbox on your device.

8. Children

P1 Companion is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used this app, contact us and we will assist with data removal.

9. Health Disclaimer

Aria is not a medical device. Her observations on coherence, stress, or breathing patterns are biofeedback tools for self-awareness — not medical diagnosis or treatment. If you are in medical distress, contact emergency services immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
