# PhiFlow Buyer-Safe Marketing Case
*Companion to `RESEARCH/first_sale_path/MASTER.md` and `docs/pilot_offer.md`.*

## Core Market Thesis

PhiFlow should be marketed first as a **research instrument**, not a broad platform.

The marketable wedge is the artifact chain:

`.phi source -> compiler/runtime output -> OpenQASM where relevant -> simulator or hardware run -> signed/dated receipt`

That chain is easier to trust than a large claim about consciousness, agents, or wellness. It gives a skeptical buyer something concrete to inspect.

## Positioning Ladder

1. **Safe first line:** Self-observing programs with reproducible receipts.
2. **Technical line:** A compiler/runtime that maps semantic constructs into auditable execution and quantum-capable workflow artifacts.
3. **Vision line:** A substrate for research into intention, observation, coherence, and agent handoff as executable semantics.

Lead with line 1 or 2. Use line 3 only after the buyer is already engaged.

## What PhiFlow Can Be Used For

### 1. Semantic Quantum Workflow Prototyping

Use PhiFlow to encode a research idea at a semantic level, then produce an OpenQASM artifact for supported quantum paths.

Buyer outcome: a reproducible research artifact, not a promise of quantum advantage.

### 2. Sensor-Conditioned Experimental Loops

Use SOMA or other telemetry as input to a controlled program flow, then record how the signal influenced runtime behavior.

Buyer outcome: an auditable sensor-to-execution loop.

### 3. Signed Agent Handoff Receipts

Use PhiFlow handoff events to preserve agent context as signed runtime evidence.

Buyer outcome: better provenance for multi-agent workflows.

### 4. Self-Observing Runtime Experiments

Use `witness` and daemon snapshots to pause, inspect, and resume execution without treating runtime state as an invisible side effect.

Buyer outcome: a testable model of adaptive execution.

### 5. Edge / Local-First Coordination Experiments

Use the MQTT resonance bus and signed events to coordinate local state without starting from a cloud-first architecture.

Buyer outcome: a prototype for local coordination with receipts.

## First Buyer Segment

Prioritize quantum / AI research teams that:

1. already use experimental software,
2. understand OpenQASM or Qiskit-style workflows,
3. care about reproducibility,
4. do not need a polished enterprise UI,
5. can judge a hardware or simulator receipt.

Do not prioritize broad wellness, clinical, or consumer markets for the first sale.

## The Offer In One Sentence

Give us one adaptive research workflow, and we will turn it into a reproducible PhiFlow receipt: source, emitted artifacts, verification notes, and a live or simulator-backed run.

## Suggested Outreach

Subject: Small research pilot: self-observing workflow -> OpenQASM/receipt

I am looking for one research team with a concrete adaptive or semantic workflow to test in PhiFlow.

PhiFlow is a research compiler/runtime for self-observing programs. The practical output is not a dashboard. It is a receipt package: `.phi` source, compiler/runtime output, OpenQASM where relevant, simulator or hardware run notes, and limitations.

If you have one workflow where observation, confidence, sensor input, or agent handoff matters, I would like to scope a fixed 6-8 week pilot around that single workflow.

## Language To Avoid

Avoid these phrases in first-touch marketing:

1. "quantum healing",
2. "proves consciousness",
3. "production-ready",
4. "guaranteed quantum advantage",
5. "medical-grade",
6. "unbreakable crypto",
7. "zero-install browser demo."

Use these instead:

1. "research-grade",
2. "artifact-backed",
3. "supported programs",
4. "receipt package",
5. "hardware attempt where access permits",
6. "signed handoff evidence",
7. "known limitations included."

## Clean Marketing Hook

**Self-observing programs with receipts.**

That is the cleanest public hook. It is short, true enough to defend, and broad enough to cover the quantum, SOMA, daemon, and handoff work without overclaiming any of them.
